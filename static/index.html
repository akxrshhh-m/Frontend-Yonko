Charter a Council Session

Council sessions follow the same law as the gala: a fixed number of chairs, a fair queue on deck, and a Den Den Mushi that rings the moment a chair is abandoned.

  • ⚖️ Fair order. First on deck, first to be called.
  • 📞 Timed offer. A ringing snail with a countdown, then it passes on.
  • 🪙 No overbooking. The chairs are counted in gold.
  • 🛡️ Full ledger. Every boarding and every departure is logged.
